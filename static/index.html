Objectness threshold

Filters weak detections. A bounding box only appears if its confidence score meets this value.

lower → more boxes · higher → confident only

NMS threshold

Non-maximum suppression merges duplicate boxes on the same object, based on their overlap (IoU).

lower → aggressive merging · higher → overlaps survive

Typical settings

General road: obj 0.40, nms 0.45. High recall: obj 0.25, nms 0.50. High precision: obj 0.60, nms 0.40.
